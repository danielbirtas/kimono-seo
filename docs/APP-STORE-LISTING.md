### Full description

**Stop guessing. Start growing.**

Kimono BI is your AI-powered business intelligence co-pilot for Shopify. Get instant visibility into what's working, which customers matter most, and where to focus next.

**Dashboard & Analytics**
See your revenue, orders, AOV, and customer metrics at a glance. Track performance over time with daily breakdowns, channel attribution, and trend comparisons.

**AI Business Advisor**
Ask questions about your store and get instant, data-driven answers. Generate comprehensive reports covering sales performance, product insights, customer behavior, and growth recommendations — all powered by AI that understands your actual store data.

**RFM Customer Segmentation** (Growth plan)
Automatically segment your customers into Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, Lost, and more. Know exactly who to target with your next campaign.

**Cohort Retention Analysis** (Growth plan)
Track how customer groups behave over time. See month-over-month retention rates and identify which acquisition periods produced your most valuable customers.

**Stock Alerts**
Set inventory thresholds and get notified before products go out of stock. Never lose a sale to poor inventory management.

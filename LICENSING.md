## 1. Open-source license (default): AGPL-3.0-only

By default, Kimono SEO is distributed under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3.0 only**. The authoritative terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE); the summary below is provided for convenience and is **not** a substitute for the license text.

### Plain-English meaning

- **You may self-host and use Kimono SEO for free**, including for commercial purposes (e.g. running it internally to do SEO for your own or your clients' stores).
- You may **study, modify, and redistribute** it, provided you keep it under AGPL-3.0 and preserve copyright and license notices.
- If you **distribute** a modified version, you must make your modified source available under the same license.

### The SaaS / network clause (Section 13) — important

AGPL is distinct from the plain GPL because of **Section 13**. If you **modify** Kimono SEO and let others interact with it **over a network** (for example, you run a modified version as a hosted SaaS product that your users log into), then:

> You must offer those users access to the **complete corresponding source code** of your modified version, under AGPL-3.0.

This is deliberate: it closes the "closed-SaaS loophole." Running an unmodified copy for your own use does not trigger any new obligation, but **turning a modified copy into a network service for others does**. If that is not acceptable for your use case, use the commercial license below.
